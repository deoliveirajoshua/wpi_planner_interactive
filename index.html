How to Use Course Visualizer

Navigation & Controls

  • Select Course: Left-click any course node in the graph, click a course in the search dropdown, or select from the department list in the sidebar.
  • Pan View: Hold Right-Click & Drag anywhere on the canvas to move around.
  • Zoom: Scroll your mouse wheel to zoom in or out.
  • Deselect / Reset: Left-click empty canvas space or click Reset View to clear selection.
  • Physics Toggle: Click Physics: OFF / ON in the toolbar to enable or disable live graph physics with a gentle attracting force between connected courses.
  • Unwind Prerequisite: In the details sidebar, click Show All (Unwind) to expand the full recursive prerequisite hierarchy tier by tier.

Interpreting the Graph

  • Red: Currently selected focus course.
  • Indigo: Prerequisites required or recommended for the selected course.
  • Blue: Courses unlocked after completing the selected course.
  • White Nodes: Background courses in the full graph overview.
